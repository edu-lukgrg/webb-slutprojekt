Duelist

Liu

Straight from Sweden, Liu charges into battle with reckless precision. A natural-born fighter with an unmatched instinct for chaos, she overwhelms enemies before they even know she's there. Play aggressive — or don't play at all.

Special Abilities

Key Ability Description
C Shadow Step EQUIP a shadow charge. FIRE to dash forward a short distance, leaving a decoy at your starting position that distracts nearby enemies for 2 seconds.
Q Flashbolt EQUIP a blinding bolt. FIRE to launch it forward. The bolt detonates on impact, flashing all enemies in its radius. ALT FIRE to curve the throw.
E Phantom Rush INSTANTLY dash to a targeted location within range. This ability recharges on each kill. Can be used to pass through thin walls and obstacles.
X Liu's Wrath (Ultimate) EQUIP your ultimate power. FIRE to unleash a sweeping energy wave across a wide area. Enemies caught in the wave are slowed and revealed for 4 seconds. Costs 7 ultimate points.

Tips for playing Liu

  • Use Shadow Step to take unexpected angles and surprise enemies.
  • Throw Flashbolt around corners with ALT FIRE before pushing — blind enemies before they can react.
  • Phantom Rush recharges on kills — play aggressively to keep the ability active.
  • Save Liu's Wrath for late-round when enemies are hiding — slow and reveal is deadly in a 1v1.
  • Combine Shadow Step with your Ultimate to enter a site and immediately lock down the entire area.
